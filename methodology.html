Research Methodology

About the Survey

My methodology was qualitative and quantitative. I conducted a survey with "select the best answer" questions for qualitative data and open-ended questions for quantitative data. The questions explored these complex concepts in sports conversations, while noting participants' gender.

In one section, each question was asked twice: once tailored to men and once tailored to women, to capture perspectives from their own gender and a different gender. This allowed me to compare the answers fairly. For example, one two-part question included: How comfortable do you feel identifying as a sports fan in front of women? How comfortable do you feel identifying as a sports fan in front of men?

Other questions included: Have you ever had your sports knowledge questioned because of your gender? What do you experience the most in sports spaces? In a few words, how would you describe a "real" sports fan?

About the Data Visualization

I used web design tactics and inspirations I researched in preparation. My goal with this site was to create engaging graphs and break down the data as simply as possible for people to truly understand the implications. To design these graphics and break down the research, I used Claude and Google Gemini AI tools to expand on what I created. I made the basic code but used AI help for design and reviewing/editing my copy.
